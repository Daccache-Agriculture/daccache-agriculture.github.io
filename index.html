07 · Climate engineering

28° cooler in the peak of August

At Nesma Agriculture in Al Kharj, Saudi Arabia, an August day reaches 50°C. Press the button to switch on the cooling system and watch the greenhouse drop to 22°C. The colours are illustrative.

AUGUST HEAT · OUTSIDE50°C
−28°CPad-and-fan cooling with boom irrigation. Nesma Agriculture lettuce project, Al Kharj, KSA.

How the desert cooling system works

  1. Fans pull outside air through wet pads.Exhaust fans at the far end create the airflow through the house.
  2. Irrigation keeps the pads saturated.Boom irrigation wets the pads, so the water can evaporate and take the heat with it.
  3. Cooled air sweeps over the crop and exits.The exhaust fans push the warm air out.
